Vegetable Stock

Description

Rich and flavorful homemade vegetable stock made with fresh aromatics, alliums, and herbs. Perfect as a base for soups, stews, and risottos.

Ingredients

  • Onions (1 large)
  • Shallots (2-3)
  • Garlic (1 head)
  • Carrots (2-3)
  • Celery (2-3)
  • Mushrooms (8 oz)
  • Roma Tomatoes (4)
  • Assorted Herbs (Thyme, Rosemary, Parsley, Bay leaves)
  • Salt (Hefty Pinch)
  • Black peppercorns (8-10)

Steps

  1. Cut ingredients into large pieces. Such as quartered or halved, depending on size.
  2. Add everything to large 8-12 quart stock pot.
  3. Fill with water until contents are covered. Or until water reaches 2-3 inches below top of pot.
  4. Bring ingredients to a slow simmer for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
  5. Strain and divide into one quart containers. Make sure to label with contents name and date.
  6. Store in refrigerator for 4-5 days or freeze for up to 6 months.
