Clams and Polenta

Description

Tender clams sautéed in a garlicky white wine sauce served over creamy polenta. This elegant yet comforting dish brings together Italian-American flavors in one beautiful plate.

Ingredients

Polenta:

  • 1 cup polenta (cornmeal)
  • 4 cups water or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt to taste

Clams:

  • 2-3 lbs littleneck clams, scrubbed
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup white wine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Lemon wedges

Steps

  1. Make the polenta: Bring water/broth to a boil, slowly whisk in polenta. Cook on low for 20-25 minutes, stirring often. Stir in butter and Parmesan. Season with salt.
  2. While polenta cooks, heat olive oil and butter in a large skillet.
  3. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, sauté for 30 seconds.
  4. Add white wine and bring to a simmer.
  5. Add clams, cover, and cook for 5-8 minutes until clams open (discard any that stay closed).
  6. Spoon creamy polenta into bowls, top with clams and sauce. Garnish with parsley and lemon.
