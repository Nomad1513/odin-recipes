Vegetable Stock

Description

Rich and flavorful homemade vegetable stock made with fresh aromatics, alliums, and herbs. Perfect as a base for soups, stews, and risottos.

Ingredients

  • 2 large onions, roughly chopped
  • 3 carrots, roughly chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, roughly chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms (cremini or button), halved
  • 2 large Roma tomatoes, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Fresh parsley sprigs (a small handful)
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 8-10 cups water

Steps

  1. Add all the chopped vegetables (onions, carrots, celery, mushrooms, tomatoes, and garlic) into a large pot.
  2. Add salt, pepper, bay leaves, parsley, and thyme.
  3. Pour in 8-10 cups of water (enough to fully cover the vegetables).
  4. Bring to a boil over high heat.
  5. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 45-60 minutes.
  6. Strain the stock through a fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth into another pot. Press gently on the vegetables to extract more flavor.
  7. Let the stock cool completely before storing or using.
  8. Store in refrigerator for 4-5 days. Or freeze for up to 6 months
