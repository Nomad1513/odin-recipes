Chicken Milanese

Description

Crispy pan-fried chicken cutlets coated in seasoned breadcrumbs and Parmesan cheese. This classic Italian dish is quick, delicious, and perfect served with a fresh arugula salad and lemon wedges.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1½ cups breadcrumbs (preferably Italian-style)
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Olive oil for frying
  • Lemon wedges for serving

Steps

  1. Place chicken breasts between plastic wrap and pound to ¼-inch thickness.
  2. Set up three bowls: flour in first, beaten eggs in second, and breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in third.
  3. Dredge each chicken cutlet in flour, then egg, then breadcrumb mixture.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Fry chicken for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate. Serve immediately with lemon wedges.
